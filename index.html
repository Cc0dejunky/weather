INFORMATION & LEGAL

Where does this data come from? +
DAISY pulls live data directly from the National Weather Service (NWS) and NOAA Public APIs. Radar imagery is provided by the Windy.com API. All meteorological data is updated every 60 seconds to ensure regional accuracy.
What triggers the Alarm? +
The audio siren is programmed to trigger when a Tornado Warning or Severe Thunderstorm Warning contains keywords such as "OBSERVED", "CONFIRMED", or "ROTATION DETECTED" AND the storm is calculated to be within 20 miles of your pinned assets.
