INFORMATION & LEGAL

Where does this data come from? +
DAISY pulls live data directly from the National Weather Service (NWS) and NOAA APIs covering the entire United States.
Predictive Logic & ETA +
The system scans NWS data for vector speeds and calculates an Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) if a storm is on a heading within 45 degrees of your pinned assets. It also searches for early warning indicators such as "TORNADO POSSIBLE" in standard thunderstorm warnings.
